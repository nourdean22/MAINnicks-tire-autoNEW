# Business - Shop Canon

This is the canonical business note for shop growth, customer experience, and owner-level priorities.

## Role Of The Shop

The shop is the main compounding engine. It should produce:

- owner take-home
- predictable customer retention
- reviews and local trust
- operational calm
- leverage for future opportunities

## Current Priorities

- Improve customer retention through follow-ups and referrals.
- Tighten Google review collection and response quality.
- Make pricing, labor, and gross-profit visibility part of the weekly review.
- Keep marketing consistent with simple content that shows trust, speed, and competence.
- Build leadership standards for team attitude, speed, and hospitality.

## Distilled Principles

- Speed matters, but not at the expense of A1 work.
- Hospitality is part of the product.
- Follow-up is a profit activity, not a side task.
- Reviews, repeat business, and customer trust are leading indicators.
- Competitive energy should become better service and better execution, not noise.

## Marketing Cadence

- 3 useful social posts per week
- before/after or in-bay proof content
- one customer-trust angle
- one speed/efficiency angle
- one education or tip angle

## Team Standards

- no bad attitude
- no clock-milking
- clear owner vs manager decisions
- smile, hospitality, and fast response
- clean work, quick work, honest work

# Mind - Discipline Canon

This is the canonical note for focus, patience, overthinking, and self-governance.

## Core Problem

The repeated pattern is fragmentation:

- too much overthinking
- boredom-driven drift
- intensity spikes instead of stable systems
- validation and comparison pulling attention away from execution

## Distilled Standard

- Do the next clear thing before searching for a better frame.
- Speak and move slower when pressure rises.
- Patience is a force multiplier, not passivity.
- Boredom tolerance is a skill.
- Focus improves when action starts before narration.

## Practices To Keep

- morning brief
- night review
- one hard thing first
- less self-monitoring during action
- direct communication
- fewer open loops

## Patterns To Watch

- asking for the perfect tool when the next action is obvious
- using harsh self-talk as fake discipline
- drifting into entertainment or prompts when tired
- chasing intensity because calm feels too plain
