## 3 · Three roadmap candidates

Each ≈ 3 months · mutually exclusive · operator picks one. Each lists 5-7 concrete features (1-2 weeks each), top 3 risks, why-it-beats-the-others, killer feature.

### Arc A · Deep observability

**Thesis:** Stop building features until you can prove the ones you have are getting better. Instrument the OS itself the way the OS instruments Nour. The system that can measure itself can compound.

**Features:**

1. **Unified Operator Cockpit (/cockpit).** One pane. Top-row scorecards (cost-today · voice-p50-latency · eval-pass-rate · brain-recall-precision · cron-success-rate). Live activity stream (SSE feed of tool-calls + cron-fires + memory-writes). 7-day deltas. Replaces 4 of the 7 scattered dashboards.
2. **Cost telemetry SLO.** Per-provider · per-task · per-conversation cost ingestion (Venice · Ollama · OpenAI · Anthropic · Cohere · Tavily · Exa · VAPI). Daily budget + alert when burn-rate forecasts overshoot. Drill-down: "this conversation cost $0.42, here's the breakdown by tool-call."
3. **Voice latency measurement loop.** Wire the v10.0.524.1 audit doc into a live metric. P50/P95 latency per layer (STT · LLM · TTS · network). Alert when sub-500ms target is breached for >3 calls in a row. Feeds VAPI apply-route as auto-tune signal.
4. **Eval regression expansion.** Extend the 35-question harness to cover: voice intent classification · brain recall precision (was the right wisdom retrieved?) · tool-call correctness (was the right tool fired?) · anti-pattern detection rate. Target: 200 golden checks by end of arc.
5. **Drift detector on the OS itself.** Daily snapshot of (route count · cron count · tool count · LOC by domain · test coverage · type-error count) → time-series → alert on regressions. Catches the silent "11 monster files growing back" pattern.
6. **Tracer-level request observability.** Every chat turn gets a request-id; trace shows (prompt-builder version → retrieval lanes → reranker scores → tool-calls → token usage → cost → latency). Operator can click any conversation and see WHY the answer was what it was.
7. **Honest-accounting dashboard.** Surface the "this said working but is broken" gap. Items shipped vs items operator-validated. Crons that fire but produce 0 useful output. Tools registered but never called.

**Top 3 risks:**

- **Observer effect bloat.** Instrumentation costs (storage · query · UI render). Mitigation: 30-day retention default · downsample after 7 days · only ingest sampled traces (1% baseline, 100% on error).
- **Vanity metrics trap.** Building dashboards Nour looks at once and never again. Mitigation: every metric must alert OR get used in an automated decision · no orphan charts.
- **Doesn't move customers/revenue.** This arc improves the OS, not the LINE OF CARS. The operator's business-DNA is FCFS revenue · this arc is upstream of that.

**Why this beats the others:** v515-v524 just shipped 24+ features with NO unified way to know if they helped. Without Arc A you're flying blind into Arc B or Arc C. Cheapest to ship (lifts heavily from existing observability dashboard + cost meter + eval harness). Highest defense-in-depth.

**Killer feature:** the Operator Cockpit. If Nour opens this every morning instead of 4 separate dashboards, the arc paid for itself.

### Arc B · Cognitive amplification

**Thesis:** The brain has 1,100+ entries but still reacts. Turn it active — proactively surface what Nour doesn't know to ask, learn his preferences from observation, mimic his style. The OS that gets sharper from use.

**Features:**

1. **Preference inference engine.** Watch every chat thumbs-up/down, every "/chill" or "/strict" toggle, every regenerate, every dismissal. Infer 8-axis preference deltas (density · verbosity · creativity · skepticism · directness · humor · jargon · structure). Apply as per-turn system-prompt addendum. Self-tunes weekly.
2. **Wisdom-suggest-at-write-time.** When the operator is composing a message in /chat or a journal entry, surface 1-2 relevant brain wisdoms inline as faded suggestions BEFORE he hits send. Killable on first dismissal. (Not ProactiveInsightCard 2.0 — different surface: it's at-write-time, not after-response.)
3. **Decision-replay coach.** Every captured decision (from `/decisions`) gets a 30-day-out automatic review: "you decided X · here's what happened · here's a Naval/Munger wisdom that fits this outcome · would you decide differently now?" Pushed to morning brief.
4. **Ghost Nick at-call-time.** The 8-axis identity overlay + 463 ChatGPT conversations get distilled into a persona vector. Chat replies are post-processed: if reply drifts >0.4 cosine from persona-vector, regenerate with persona-anchor in prompt.
5. **Proactive contradiction surfacing.** The brain already detects contradictions (per `brain_learning_architecture.md`). Wire them: when operator says X today that contradicts Y from 30 days ago, surface BOTH at next chat turn and ask "which is current?" Updates the canonical belief.
6. **Anticipated-question feed.** Daily cron: based on operator's last 7 days of chat + decisions + journal + open commitments, draft the 3 questions he's most likely to ask tomorrow. Pre-compute the answers. When he asks, instant response.
7. **Operator-style mimicry in outputs.** Apply preference vector to non-chat outputs too: morning brief tone · Telegram push wording · email drafts · journal-entry summaries. One consistent voice across surfaces.

**Top 3 risks:**

- **Slop slope.** ProactiveInsightCard was killed for a reason. Proactive features are AI-slop-magnets. Mitigation: every proactive surface must be killable on dismissal · must beat a "no-suggestion" baseline in eval regression · must be measured for actually-clicked-through rate.
- **Privacy gradient.** Inference engine watches everything. Mitigation: all preference data stays in operator's Neon, never leaves; preference vector is opaque (don't show "we think you're more direct this week" — too creepy).
- **Persona drift compounding.** Cosine-anchor to Ghost Nick could collapse the model into stiff repetition. Mitigation: anchor threshold tunable; eval regression catches collapse early.

**Why this beats the others:** uniquely exploits the existing brain corpus + the 8-axis identity + Ghost Nick — assets that exist but are underused. Highest "I didn't know that" / "that's smart" density (per power_control directive). Maximizes return on the 1,100-entry investment.

**Killer feature:** Anticipated-question feed. If Nour wakes up to 3 pre-computed answers to questions he was about to ask, the OS has graduated from "tool" to "second brain."

## 4 · Recommended arc

**Recommend Arc A · Deep observability.** With caveats stated honestly.

### Reasoning

- **v515-v524 just shipped 24+ features.** The eval regression harness (#7) is the only thing measuring whether any of them helped. Shipping Arc B or Arc C ON TOP OF that unmeasured base compounds risk. ELON-first-principles 5-step says STEP 1 = question every requirement: we can't honestly question what we just built without instrumentation.
- **Operator's standing-directive line _"comprehensive QA assessments"_ + _"regular progress checkpoints to avoid surprises"_** maps cleanly onto observability. Arc B's "proactive everything" risks reintroducing the AI-slop that v10.0.484 just killed. Arc C's cross-repo coupling violates feedback_business_separation.
- **Lift from existing.** Arc A reuses 4 existing pillars (observability dashboard at `/system/observability` · request tracer · cost meter · eval regression harness). Arc B requires a new at-write-time UI surface + persona-vector training pipeline. Arc C requires bridge rewrite. Arc A is the cheapest 10x.
- **Pays for the next two arcs.** Once cost telemetry, voice latency loop, drift detector, and eval expansion are wired, Arc B's "proactive everything" has a way to PROVE its lift, and Arc C's cross-domain decisions have a way to PROVE their revenue impact. Without Arc A, B and C are gambling.

### Honest trade-offs (don't soft-pedal)

- **Arc A does not move LINE-OF-CARS.** The operator's #1 business KPI is untouched for 3 months. This is real and painful.
- **Arc A is the LEAST visually exciting.** It's dashboards and traces. No new chat behavior, no new agent. The operator's "less static, more alive" directive is HARDER to meet here than in Arc B.
- **Arc A risks "instrumentation church"** — building so much measurement that the team becomes data-analysts instead of system-builders. Mitigation: every metric must alert OR feed a decision; orphan charts get culled monthly.
- **If the operator's priority is LIFE outcomes (weight · marriage · faith · health), Arc C's wellbeing slice would beat Arc A.** That's a real signal the operator should weigh.

### When to override

If the operator says "shop revenue is on fire" or "I want to lose 30 lbs by Sep 1" → go Arc C. If the operator says "I'm bored, build me something delightful" → go Arc B. If neither, Arc A.
