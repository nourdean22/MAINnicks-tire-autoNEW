> Map gaps 6 ("Home skips missions and the journal") and 8 ("chat-to-mission links are written and never read"), Sunsama S3/S4, Tana T3.
>
> - **Home.** The brief's lead row names the mission and goal behind the next move (`leadContext`, one `task.findUnique`; the task's own goal overrides the mission's, the inbox mission is not named). A Journal line shows the newest entry (people page's `journalExcerpt`) with its live people as dossier links. Both are `"unknown"` on a failed read and say so.
> - **Journal day view.** Each day header in the feed opens "Done and contacts": that ET day's task completions (with their goal) and relationship-ledger contact rows (`contactRowsOnly`), from `lib/services/journal-day.ts` `dayActivity`, the day-bounded helper beside `relatedActivity`. Each read is named in `failed` when it throws and in `truncated` when it fills its cap (newest rows kept). A date the month does not have is a BAD_REQUEST. Situation, decision and retro rows in the feed are now keyed by their ET day like dumps, so the panel reads the day the header shows.
> - **Weekly review.** `/journal?review=week`: per active goal, tasks done (read only for the reviewed goals) and grounded entries since the last review (7 days with none, capped at 28); a note per goal saved as a journal entry grounded to that goal (confidence 1, confirmed, `enrichedAt` stamped, no AI call), or ungrounded when the goal left the active list, never dropped; "Extend a week" / "Close" on WEEK goals through `updateGoal`. One serializable transaction keyed by the form's `reviewId` (stored in `extractedItems`): a retry after a lost response returns the saved rows and repeats no write or goal action. A failed side read is named in each goal block. Goal actions match ids against the server's active list; text is never parsed for ids. The journal brain's dangling-link sweep and its re-grounding write skip review rows (`lib/brain/journal-review-marker.ts`), so the model never re-grounds or retypes them.
> - **Missions.** Each expanded mission shows up to 3 linked chats from `chat_conversations.mission_id` (the `conversation-mission-link` cron's first reader), one query for the board, linking `/chat?cid=`. A capped read or a mission past the 100-id limit says "not read", never "no chats".
