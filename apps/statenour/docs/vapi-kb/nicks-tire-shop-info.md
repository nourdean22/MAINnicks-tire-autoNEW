## How We Operate

### First-come-first-serve · drop-off preferred

Nick's runs **first-come-first-serve**. There are no booked appointments.
Walk-ins welcome 7 days a week. Drop-offs are the preferred path because
they free up the customer's whole day — drop the car, leave, we work at
our own pace, we call when it's done.

- **Same-day service** if dropped off before 10 AM.
- A "scheduled drop-off" through the website holds your place in line —
  it does NOT reserve a time slot.

### Pit Stop Tire Experience (the differentiator)

For tire-only work, customers can pull up in front of any bay and **stay
in their car**. The manager comes to the window, asks how to help, and
4-5 dedicated tire techs handle the work outside — rain, snow, sun, fog.
Manager brings the receipt and any change out to the car. In and out in
**under 20 minutes**.

### Drop-off + Uber-out flywheel

Drop the car off, call an Uber out (one tap from nickstire.org), we work,
we call when done, customer Ubers back. We run a ride at no cost for
regulars.

### Premium wait treatment (when waiting is unavoidable)

Water, coffee, phone chargers, loaner reading. Honest live time updates.

## FAQ

### "Do I need an appointment?"

No. We're first-come-first-serve. Walk-ins welcome 7 days a week. If you
want to skip the line, drop your car off — same-day service if it's in
before 10 AM.

### "How much for [service]?"

We can't quote that over the phone — every vehicle is a little different.
Bring it in for a free written estimate, or we can have someone from the
shop call you back to talk it through.

### "How long will it take?"

Tires only: 20 minutes typical (Pit Stop service · stay in your car).
Bigger jobs: drop off, we'll call you when it's done. Same-day if it's
in before 10 AM.

### "Do you finance?"

Yes. $0-down financing on big-ticket repairs through SNAP, Afterpay,
Acima, Koalafi, and American First Finance. Pre-approval is fast.

### "Do you do alignments?"

Yes. Full Hunter alignment with printed before-and-after numbers.

### "Do you handle E-Check / emissions?"

Yes. We can run E-Check + diagnose / repair anything that's failing.

### "Do you do oil changes?"

Yes. Standard oil + filter, synthetic blend, full synthetic. Drop off
or stay — usually under 30 minutes.

### "Spanish?"

For Spanish callers: collect name and number, then a Spanish-speaking
team member calls back. We don't have a live Spanish line in real time
during all hours.

### "Are you open today?"

Yes if it's Monday–Saturday between 8 AM and 6 PM, or Sunday between
9 AM and 4 PM. Open 7 days a week.

### "Where are you located?"

17625 Euclid Avenue, in Euclid, near the Amazon warehouse. Cleveland
OH 44112.

### "Free inspection?"

We have a free inspection under 1 hour for tires, brakes, basic
visual / fluid checks. Deep inspections that pull a wheel or scope a
specific symptom are $50, and that $50 credits toward the repair if
you go ahead with the work.

### "Do you take walk-ins?"

Yes. We're first-come-first-serve · walk-ins welcome 7 days a week
during open hours. If you have a tight schedule, drop the car off
and we'll get you back on the road faster.

### "Can I drop off after hours?"

Yes. Park in the lot, drop the keys in the slot by the side door,
and text or call (216) 862-0005 with your info. We'll start on it
first thing the next morning.

### "Do you do tires for trucks / SUVs / vans?"

Yes. We handle passenger, light truck, SUV, and van tires · all major
brands available through our Gateway Tire ordering. Drop by or
call (216) 862-0005 and we'll match the right tire to your vehicle.

### "Do you do state inspections / E-Check?"

Yes. We run Ohio E-Check + can diagnose / repair anything that's
failing. Bring the vehicle in.

### "How long does an oil change take?"

Standard oil change is usually under 30 minutes if you wait. Drop
off is faster · call before 10 AM and you'll have it back same day.

### "Do you take credit cards?"

Yes · Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express. We also take
cash, SNAP Finance, AFTERPAY, Acima, Koalafi, and American First
Finance. $0-down financing on big-ticket repairs.

### "Do you do AC repair?"

Yes. Recharge, leak detection, compressor replacement · full AC
service. Bring the vehicle in for a free written estimate.

### "Do you have a waiting area?"

Yes · water, coffee, phone chargers, loaner reading. For anything
over 30 minutes of work, we recommend dropping off · we'll call an
Uber for you (one tap from nickstire.org) so you don't waste your
day in the waiting room.

### "Do you fix exhaust / muffler issues?"

Yes. Welding repairs, full replacements, pipe / muffler / catalytic
work. Bring it in for a free estimate.

### "Are you the cheapest?"

We're not the cheapest · we're the most honest. We don't surprise-
charge, we honor 36-month warranties, and we tell you exactly what
the vehicle needs (and what it doesn't). The shop down the street
might quote less but charge more after the work starts. We give
you the written number first.

### "Can you handle European / luxury brands?"

Yes. We work on most makes and models · BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Lexus,
Acura, Volkswagen, etc. Some specialty parts may need to be ordered.
Bring the vehicle in for a free diagnostic.

### "What if you don't have my exact tire size used?"

If the exact size isn't on the rack AND your vehicle is NOT
all-wheel-drive (so 2WD, FWD, or RWD), we can usually substitute a
**closely-compatible size** · the next size up or down that fits
the wheel and matches load + speed rating. Customer saves money,
no safety risk, vehicle drives normally.

**AWD vehicles are different** · all four tires must match in size
AND tread depth so the differential doesn't bind. We do NOT
substitute sizes on AWD · if we don't have the exact size in used,
we'll find one or order new.

The manager confirms compatibility on every used-tire substitution ·
this is a "manager picks the right tire" situation, not a website-
configurable thing.
