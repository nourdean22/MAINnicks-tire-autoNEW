⚡ Install NOUR OS

Add NOUR OS to your iPhone home screen. One tap to log your day.

iPhone (Safari)

1 Open Safari on your iPhone (not Chrome — it must be Safari)
2 Go to your-ip:3000/m (or your deployed URL)
3 Tap the Share button (box with arrow at the bottom)
4 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5 Name it "NOUR OS" and tap Add
Open NOUR OS →

Works on same Wi-Fi as your computer. No app store needed.
