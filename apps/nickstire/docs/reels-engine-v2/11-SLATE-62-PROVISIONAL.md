## How to read a row

- Slot 1 = discovery (curiosity, broad). Slot 2 = trust (credibility, usefulness, local). These are the Discovery and Trust roles of `10-FLAGSHIP-PRODUCTION.md` (Slots and posting time).
- Pack marker: `[R]` in the daily rotation; `[X]` in `ROTATION_EXCLUDED`. An `[X]` pack is cut by hand from capture (`10-FLAGSHIP-PRODUCTION.md`, Production route) or has its beats rewritten to name the camera subject before enqueue.
- Evidence class (the slate's vocabulary, not a repo enum): SHOP = VERIFIED SHOP EVIDENCE (a real job's defect or reading); FACT = VERIFIED EXTERNAL FACT (a cited packet or regulator rule carries the payoff); DEMO = REPRESENTATIVE DEMONSTRATION (a test done on any car or scrap part, not a customer's fault); SYNTH = SYNTHETIC ILLUSTRATION (generated beats; never presented as documentation of real work).
- Route is the angle's route from `angle-bank.json`: `real`, `real+det` (real plus a deterministic card). No placed angle is routed `det` or `ai_illustrative`.
- Ready: `footage-needed` = the slot is committed to its real route and the pool holds no clip for it (0 credits, waits on capture). `brief-ready` = a rotation pack the builder accepts; the lane can render it generated today at 12 credits per generated beat (60 for a 5-beat pack, 72 for a 6-beat pack; beats counted the builder's way, `storyboardBeats` > `beats` > `storyboard`, `approvedReelPackRotation.ts`; `09-90-DAY-MODEL.md`: 90 of 99 rotation packs have 5 beats; `10-FLAGSHIP-PRODUCTION.md`: about 60 credits per Reel at five clips); its real route is the 0-credit upgrade once its clip group is captured. Rule used: every pilot, flagship and `[X]` slot, every family B or C slot (`03-FAMILIES-AND-STANDARD.md`: a matched pair needs one camera; "none for the number"), the two number-based family D slots, plus A049 (D08-2, cut from the D02-2 brake job) are footage-needed.
- Clips = capture-day clip group (`10-FLAGSHIP-PRODUCTION.md`): puncture P1-P8, balance B1-B6, tread pair T1-T6, brake (A004's pilot clips, `08-PILOT.md`), scan tool (A007's clip 3), atmosphere (the ten shop clips + audio), none (not a capture-day group; its own clip on a later job). The group names the station and kit; only A003, A002, A008, A027 and part of A001 are served by the capture-day clips themselves.
- Dur: real cuts 18-30 s; generated 23 s for a 5-beat pack and 27 s for a 6-beat pack (one 4 s clip per beat plus the 3 s freeze, `REEL_OUTPUT_RULES.saveFreezeSeconds` in `client/src/lib/facelessReelStudio.ts`; the lane's band is 18-27 s, `09-90-DAY-MODEL.md`). Ask: one per Reel, written as a declarable kind (`ReelAskKind` in `shared/reelAsk.ts`: `profile`, `dm`, `save`, `visit`; a "send this to" prompt is a caption signal under `save`, not a kind of its own). 30 of the 31 brief-ready packs and all 6 substitutes declare no ask (only D30-2 declares `save`), so a lane render today has no end card (`resolveReelAsk` returns null, `reelAsk.ts`; the builder never defaults it, `approvedReelPackRotation.ts`); the ask becomes real only when `ask` is added to that pack's `brief.json` (operator-approved edit). Keywords other than TREAD, SHAKE, NAIL (`08-PILOT.md`) are proposals, not anything a pack declares; the proof packs declare `visit` today (`09-90-DAY-MODEL.md`), so the end card changes before admission if the keyword stands.
- Memory devices (`10-FLAGSHIP-PRODUCTION.md`): footage-needed slots carry the pointer at the reveal, the spoken line and the capture-day sound; brief-ready slots can carry the sound only after capture day and never the pointer.
- Uncited truth packets (`sources: []` in `shared/mechanicalTruth.ts`): `vibration`, `pothole_damage`, `no_start`. They gate publish, not the slate; affected slots say so in the Variation cell.
