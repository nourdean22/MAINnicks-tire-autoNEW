# __SECTION 1: COMPLIANCE RULES__

## __1.1 — Acima Is NOT Financing__

__The #1 compliance rule: __Acima is a lease-to-own (rental purchase) agreement. It is NOT a loan, NOT credit, NOT financing. Acima must never be described as, or grouped with, financing or credit options. This is Acima’s most critical merchant requirement.

## __1.2 — Required Disclaimer (Must Appear Everywhere)__

The following disclaimer MUST appear on every page, ad, or material that mentions Acima:

__REQUIRED DISCLAIMER:__

*\*The advertised service is a rental or lease-purchase agreement provided by Acima. It is not a loan, credit, or financing. While no credit history is required, Acima obtains information from consumer reporting agencies in connection with lease applications. Acquiring ownership by leasing costs more than the retailer’s cash price. Not available in MN, NJ, WI, or WY.*

This disclaimer must be visible, readable, and not hidden behind a click, dropdown, or scroll. The Progressive Leasing case specifically cited hiding cost disclosures behind a non-descript dropdown arrow as deceptive.

## __1.3 — Pricing Transparency Rules__

The FTC’s Consumer Leasing Act and Regulation M require specific disclosures when you advertise lease payment amounts. If you show ANY specific payment amount (a “trigger term”), you must ALSO disclose:

1. The total of all payments required to acquire ownership
2. That the total cost to own via leasing is MORE than the cash price
3. The 90-day same-as-cash option and its terms
4. The number and frequency of scheduled payments
5. Whether a security deposit or initial payment is required

__What this means practically: __If you put “As low as $25/week” on the website, you MUST also show the total cost of ownership and a clear statement that leasing costs more than paying cash. You cannot show the attractive weekly number without the full picture.

## __1.4 — What You CAN Safely Say__

These phrases are compliant when paired with the required disclaimer:

- "No credit needed\*" (with disclaimer asterisk)
- "Lease-to-own options available\*"
- "Get what you need today — lease-to-own with flexible payments\*"
- "90-day same-as-cash option available\*"
- "Apply in minutes — no credit history required\*"
- "Shop without using credit\*"
- "Flexible lease payment options for auto repair\*"

The asterisk must link to or be immediately followed by the full Acima disclaimer.

## __1.5 — What You CANNOT Say__

These claims are prohibited or high-risk:

- “No credit check” — Acima DOES obtain consumer reporting data; say “no credit history required” instead
- “0% interest” or “no interest” — leases don’t have interest rates; this implies it’s a loan
- “Financing available” when referring to Acima specifically
- “Same as cash” without clarifying the 90-day window and conditions
- Any specific payment amount without full cost-of-ownership disclosure
- Implying Acima approval is guaranteed (“everyone is approved”)
- Grouping Acima in a list with Synchrony, Snap, or Koalafi under a “financing” header

## __1.6 — Google & Platform Compliance__

Risks from Google, social media, and ad platforms:

### __Google Business Profile:__

- Do NOT list “lease-to-own” as a separate service on GBP — it’s a payment option, not a service
- Do NOT create a separate GBP listing for financing/leasing
- You CAN mention payment flexibility in your GBP description
- Posts about Acima are fine as long as disclaimers are included

### __Google Ads:__

- Acima lease-to-own is NOT classified as a financial product under Google Ads policy (it’s a lease, not a loan)
- However, if your ad copy triggers Google’s financial services classifier (words like “financing,” “credit,” “loan”), your ads may be flagged or require financial services verification
- Safest approach: Use “lease-to-own” and “flexible payments” language in ad copy, never “financing”
- Always include your physical business address (required for financial-adjacent ads)

### __Facebook / Meta Ads:__

- Meta’s Special Ad Categories include “Credit” — if your ad is about obtaining credit, you must use the credit category with restricted targeting
- Acima is NOT credit, so you should NOT need the credit category — but avoid language that could be miscategorized
- Safe: “Lease-to-own auto repair — no credit needed” with disclaimer
- Risky: “Get approved for financing” — triggers credit category and misrepresents Acima

## __1.7 — Ohio State Requirements__

Ohio Revised Code Chapter 1351 governs lease-purchase agreements. Key requirements:

- Lease-purchase agreements must include a description of the property (the auto repair service)
- Must disclose total of all payments necessary to acquire ownership
- Must disclose the cash price of the service
- Ohio does NOT require APR disclosure on lease-to-own (unlike credit transactions)
- Acima handles the actual lease agreement and disclosures — your job as the merchant is to not misrepresent what Acima is
