# VAPI call-mix measurement — 2026-07-26

**Method:** read-only aggregate SELECTs against production `vapi_call_logs`, main-loop,
no PII emitted (counts and percentages only). Window: 90 days (the table's full history —
first row 2026-05-20, so 90d == all rows).

**Why:** the repo's "~60% of inbound calls are used-tire inquiries" drove the entire
tire-first VAPI architecture, but it lived in a May-2026 source comment, not a demand
report. This was an attempt to verify it.

---

## Verdict on the ~60% claim: **UNVERIFIABLE with current instrumentation**

Not confirmed, not refuted. The two available signals cannot answer the question:

| Signal | Value | Why it cannot settle the claim |
|---|---|---|
| `serviceMention` | `tire` 1365 · `brake` 198 · **nothing else** | The field is effectively **binary**. 1365 + 198 = 1563 = every substantive call. Anything not classified brake becomes "tire", so "87% tire" measures the classifier's label set, not customer demand. |
| Keyword over `aiSummary` | "tire (any)" 97.4% | `aiSummary` is written by a **tire-first assistant**. It says "tire" in nearly every summary regardless of what the caller wanted. This measures the assistant's vocabulary, not the customer's need. |

**Explicit `used tire` mention: 213 / 1563 = 13.6%.** Treat as a *floor*, not an estimate —
summaries need not use the word "used". It is well below 60%, but the instrument is too
weak to call the claim refuted.

**To actually measure it** you need customer-turn classification over transcripts, not
summary keywords. `transcriptUrl` exists on the table; that is the path.

### The channel-priority finding

**Voice carries ~100× the inbound volume of SMS.**

- Voice: **2,094 calls / 90 days**
- SMS: **58 customer turns / 180 days** (ROS-058 audit, 2026-07-25)

Any conversation-quality work that targets SMS first is optimising the smaller channel by
two orders of magnitude. This is the strongest prioritisation signal in the dataset and it
argues for voice-first work — which also matches where the 40.3% forward rate sits.

## Honest limits of this measurement

- **One table, no transcripts.** Intent was inferred from a summary field, not customer speech.
- **`serviceMention` is not a taxonomy.** Two labels total; it cannot express "logistics",
  "status", "financing" or "complaint", so those are invisible here.
- **No inbound/outbound split** was applied; the table may include outbound calls.
- **No dedupe by caller.** A repeat caller counts once per call.
- **90d == all history.** No trend baseline exists before 2026-05-20.
- Percentages are of the ≥20s denominator (1,563) unless stated.

## Recommended next step

Do **not** re-anchor the architecture on any percentage from this table. Either:

1. Classify customer turns from `transcriptUrl` (real answer, more work), or
2. Widen `serviceMention` into a real label set going forward and re-measure in 30 days.

Until then the tire-first design should be treated as an **unverified working hypothesis**
(as now marked in `server/services/vapi.ts`), not an evidence-backed decision.
