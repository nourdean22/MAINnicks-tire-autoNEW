## 1 · The penny test — HIGGSFIELD ONLY (Veo RAI-blocked)

> Your tires can look perfect and still be unsafe.
>
> Put a penny in the tread, head facing down.
> If you can see the top of the head, you are below 2/32" — legally worn.
>
> On wet Euclid roads that is the difference between stopping and sliding.
>
> 📌 Save this and check all four before your next long drive.
>
> #clevelandohio #euclidohio #tiresafety #cartips

## 2 · Squealing vs grinding brakes

> Grinding when you brake is not "almost time." It is past time.
>
> Squealing is usually the wear indicator doing its job — an early warning.
> Grinding is metal on metal. You are cutting into the rotor right now.
>
> One is a brake job. The other is a brake job plus rotors.
>
> Send this to someone whose car is making one of these sounds.
>
> #brakes #cartips #clevelandohio #carmaintenance

## 3 · Cold weather and the tire light

> Every 10 degrees the temperature drops takes about 1 PSI out of every tire.
>
> That is why the light appears on the first cold Cleveland morning and you did
> not run over anything.
>
> Check the pressure cold, before you drive. Warm tires read high and hide it.
>
> 📌 Save this for the first frost.
>
> #tirepressure #ohioweather #cartips #clevelandohio

## 4 · Alignment vs balance

> These get mixed up constantly, and they are different repairs.
>
> Shaking at highway speed is usually balance.
> Pulling to one side, or tires wearing on one edge, is usually alignment.
>
> Knowing which one you have keeps you from paying for both.
>
> 📌 Save this before your next appointment.
>
> #wheelalignment #cartips #clevelandohio #autorepair

## 5 · How far you can drive on a spare

> A compact spare is not a tire. It is a ride to the shop.
>
> Most are rated around 50 miles and under 50 mph.
> They have less tread, less grip, and no wear indicator.
>
> Driving on one all week is how a flat becomes a tow.
>
> 📌 Save this now, before you need it on the shoulder.
>
> #flattire #roadside #cartips #clevelandohio

## 6 · Tires have an expiration date

> Tread is not the only thing that wears out. Rubber ages.
>
> There is a four digit date code on the sidewall. First two digits are the
> week, last two are the year.
>
> A tire with deep tread can still be too old to trust.
>
> 📌 Save this and go read your sidewall.
>
> #tiresafety #cartips #clevelandohio #carmaintenance

## 7 · Solid vs flashing check engine light

> Solid and flashing mean completely different things.
>
> Solid means get it looked at soon.
> Flashing means stop driving. It usually points to a misfire that can destroy
> the catalytic converter — one of the most expensive parts on the car.
>
> 📌 Save this. The difference is thousands of dollars.
>
> #checkenginelight #cartips #autorepair #clevelandohio

## 8 · What a bad wheel bearing sounds like

> A humming that changes when you steer is not your tires.
>
> Wheel bearing noise gets louder when you turn one way and quieter the other,
> because you are shifting load onto the bad side.
>
> Tire noise stays the same through the turn.
>
> 📌 Save this and test it on your next drive.
>
> #cartips #autorepair #clevelandohio #carmaintenance

## 9 · Oil change intervals

> The 3,000 mile sticker is a sales tool, not an engineering spec.
>
> Your owner's manual has the real interval for your engine and oil type.
> For most modern cars it is considerably longer.
>
> Short trips and cold winters shorten it. Highway miles extend it.
>
> 📌 Save this and check your manual before the next sticker.
>
> #oilchange #cartips #carmaintenance #clevelandohio

## 10 · Pothole damage you cannot see

> The pothole that did not flatten your tire may still have cost you.
>
> Impact can bend a wheel, knock out alignment, or bruise the sidewall from the
> inside where nothing shows.
>
> The tell is new pulling, new shaking, or a slow leak that starts days later.
>
> 📌 Save this. Cleveland roads are not done with us.
>
> #potholes #clevelandohio #euclidohio #cartips

## 11 · All-season vs winter tires

> All-season is a compromise, and it stops working around 45 degrees.
>
> Below that the rubber compound hardens and grip drops — on dry pavement too,
> not just snow.
>
> Winter tires stay soft. That is the whole difference.
>
> 📌 Save this for when the forecast turns.
>
> #wintertires #ohioweather #clevelandohio #winterdriving

## 12 · Battery warnings before it strands you

> A battery rarely dies without warning. The warnings are just quiet.
>
> Slow crank on cold mornings. Headlights that dim at idle. Needing a jump once.
>
> Most batteries last three to five years. Cleveland winters are hard on them.
>
> 📌 Save this if yours is over three years old.
>
> #carbattery #wintercar #cartips #clevelandohio

## 13 · Why the car pulls to one side

> A pull has more than one cause, and they are not equally urgent.
>
> Pulling that changes with braking points at the brakes.
> Pulling that is constant points at alignment or uneven tire pressure.
>
> Check pressure first. It is free and it is often the answer.
>
> 📌 Save this before you book anything.
>
> #cartips #wheelalignment #autorepair #clevelandohio

## 14 · Cabin filter vs engine air filter

> Your car has two air filters and they do different jobs.
>
> The engine filter protects the engine.
> The cabin filter cleans the air you breathe — and a clogged one is usually why
> the heat or AC feels weak.
>
> 📌 Save this if your vents lost their punch.
>
> #cartips #carmaintenance #clevelandohio #euclidohio

## 15 · What "you need struts" actually means

> This one gets quoted a lot and explained almost never.
>
> Struts control how the car settles after a bump. Worn ones let it keep
> bouncing, which lengthens stopping distance and chews tires unevenly.
>
> The test: push down hard on one corner. It should rebound once and settle.
>
> 📌 Save this and try it in your driveway.
>
> #suspension #cartips #autorepair #clevelandohio

## 16 · Tread depth for rain vs snow

> 2/32" is the legal minimum. It is not the safe one.
>
> Below 4/32" you lose meaningful grip in snow.
> Below 3/32" hydroplaning risk climbs sharply in rain.
>
> Legal and safe are two different numbers.
>
> 📌 Save both numbers. They matter at different times of year.
>
> #tiresafety #cartips #winterdriving #clevelandohio

## 17 · Tire rotation

> Rotation is the cheapest way to make tires last longer.
>
> Front and rear tires wear at different rates and in different patterns.
> Moving them evens it out — often adding thousands of miles to the set.
>
> Every 5,000 to 7,500 miles is the usual window.
>
> 📌 Save this and check when yours were last rotated.
>
> #tirerotation #carmaintenance #cartips #clevelandohio

## 18 · Coolant color

> The color of what is under your car narrows the problem fast.
>
> Green, orange or pink usually means coolant.
> Clear and odorless is usually just AC condensation, and it is normal.
>
> Coolant on the ground is not something to watch for a few weeks.
>
> 📌 Save this for the next mystery puddle in your driveway.
>
> #cartips #autorepair #carmaintenance #clevelandohio

## 19 · Noises that mean stop driving now

> Most car noises can wait. These cannot.
>
> Grinding when braking. Knocking that speeds up with the engine.
> A rhythmic clunk that tracks with wheel speed.
>
> Each of these gets dramatically more expensive with every mile you add.
>
> 📌 Save this one. It is the one you will want at the worst moment.
>
> #cartips #autorepair #carsafety #clevelandohio

## 20 · Questions to ask before authorizing any repair

> You are allowed to ask these. A good shop will not flinch.
>
> Can you show me the part? What happens if I wait? Is this safety or
> maintenance? Can I have the old part back?
>
> Any shop that will not answer plainly is telling you something.
>
> 📌 Save this and bring it to your next appointment. Anywhere, not just here.
>
> #autorepair #cartips #clevelandohio #euclidohio
