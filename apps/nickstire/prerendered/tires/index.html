Used Tires Euclid & Cleveland — Inspected, Installed, Honest

"Used tires" earned a bad name because most lots sell whatever rolls in. We don't. Every casing has to clear all four gates below before it's allowed on the rack — the ones that fail get scrapped, not discounted. Same professional installation, same included mount/balance/valve stems/disposal — just a friendlier number on the receipt. Payment programs on the spot if you need them.

Select 12-inch sizes from $25 installed; most standard passenger sizes $40–$80 installed.

1 · Deep Tread

Absolute minimum of 5/32″ to 7/32″ verified depth — measured with a gauge, not eyeballed. New tires start at 10/32″; Ohio's legal bald limit is 2/32″. Our floor sits far above it.

2 · Sidewall Scan

Manual and ultrasonic inspection of both sidewalls for bubbles, dry rot, and tire-bead tearing. A sidewall defect can't be repaired — one flaw and the tire is scrapped.

3 · DOT Production Gate

We read the DOT date code on every casing. Zero tires accepted if the code is older than 5 years — rubber oxidizes from the inside out no matter how much tread is left.

4 · Internal Plug Integrity

We dismount and inspect the inner liner directly. Prior punctures must be proper plug-patch repairs in the tread zone. No sidewall patches, ever — those tires don't get sold.

Fail any one point and the tire never touches your car. Ask the counter to show you the tread gauge reading and DOT code on your set before we mount it — we'll walk you through it.
