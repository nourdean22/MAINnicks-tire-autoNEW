Warning Signs & Symptoms

DOES YOUR CAR HAVE THESE BRAKE WARNING SIGNS?

Problems starting, warning lights, unusual sounds or behaviors? Don't wait until a minor symptom turns into a major breakdown.

Squeaking Brakes

A high-pitched squeak or squeal when applying brakes is usually the wear indicator telling you the pads are getting thin and need inspection.

Grinding Brakes

A harsh metal-on-metal grinding sound means the pads are completely worn through and are scoring the rotors. Seek inspection immediately to prevent severe damage.

Soft or Spongy Brake Pedal

If the brake pedal feels mushy or goes almost to the floor, you may have air in the brake lines, a hydraulic leak, or low brake fluid. Do not drive on a soft pedal.

Vehicle Pulling While Braking

If the vehicle pulls to one side when you brake, it could indicate a seized caliper, uneven pad wear, or a collapsed brake hose.

Brake Vibration & Shaking

A pulsating or vibrating sensation in the brake pedal or steering wheel during stops is a classic sign of warped brake rotors.

Longer Stopping Distances

If you feel your vehicle is taking longer to halt, your brake pads may have lost their friction capability due to excessive wear or glazing.

Brake Warning Lights

An illuminated red brake light or amber ABS light on the dashboard indicates a system diagnostic code or low fluid pressure. Get it scanned.

Burning Smells

A sharp, chemical burning smell after heavy braking suggests overheated pads or a seized caliper dragging on the rotor.
