About Nick's

Serving Cleveland's East Side since 2018. An independent shop built on one idea: show you the problem before we fix it.

Built from the ground up on Euclid Ave.

Nick's Tire & Auto has served East Side Cleveland from the same Euclid Ave corner since 2018 — many neighbors first knew the shop as Moe's Tire & Auto. Same owner, same corner: we simply changed the name to Nick's. The mission never changed — give East Side drivers a shop they could actually trust. No pressure sales. No mystery invoices. Just honest answers, fair prices, and the respect of showing you exactly what's wrong before we touch your car.

What started as a small independent shop has grown into Cleveland's 4.9★, 1,715-review East Side shop — real customers, real reviews, no marketing budget.

We're not a chain. We're not a dealership. We're your neighbors — and we treat your car like it belongs to one.

In writing, on every receipt

Four guarantees we put in print.

01

12-month parts / 90-day labor warranty

Failed part or improper repair? Bring it back, no charge — full re-do, including labor.

02

Quoted price = paid price

If the written estimate is $440, the bill is $440. If we find something extra, we stop and call you with the new number — no surprise add-ons at pickup.

03

We don't replace what isn't broken

We test before we replace. If the part on your car still works, we say so — even when replacing it would make us money.

04

If we can't fix it, you don't pay

If we mis-diagnose and the issue isn't resolved, the diagnostic and repair are free. We eat the cost — it's our problem, not yours.

Every receipt has these printed on the back. Not because the law makes us — because we want you holding us to them.
