## Recurring patterns → prevention

1. **Silent failure reporting success** (security-scan zeros; historic cron/webhook cases): any
   tool whose output is a safety signal must fail loudly on parse/capture failure — never default-zero.
2. **Description-as-enforcement** (arsenalNotebookLM): LLM tool contracts need code-level
   allowlists, not prose.
3. **Unbounded external awaits** (reel render; historic vapiFetch/nourOsQuote): every network/subprocess
   await needs a wall-clock bound — `withTimeout` exists in `@nour/utils`.
4. **Shared mutable files under concurrency** (moneyprinter config.toml): per-run copies or
   single-flight; always atomic tmp+rename.
