Source: two Perplexity Pro research passes run in Chrome on 2026-10-26 (07:45 ET, 77 sources: camera stacks; 07:46 ET, 52
sources: audio capture and watchdogs), then each claim checked against its linked doc by a read-only skeptic the same day.
Labels: VERIFIED = the linked doc says it. RESEARCH = the research said it, the linked page does not (treat as a lead).

## Counting and timing cars

| Job | Best mechanism | Doc | Our gap | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Count an arrival | VERIFIED: Frigate zones test the bottom-centre of the box; MQTT `frigate/events` carry the tracked id, boxes, `current_zones`, `entered_zones`. RESEARCH: `entered_zones` is membership, so keep the order of zone changes yourself. INFERRED: accept an ordered approach-then-inside transition, or a stable side change across a directed line, as ONE entry per track, with reverse-motion and retrack guards | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/zones/ , https://docs.frigate.video/integrations/mqtt/ | Prior art exists: the edge counts portal crossings (`camera-bridge/edge_main.py`, crossing line in `camera-bridge/docs/NICKSMAX-SIGN-LENS.md`) and visitd runs a dwell state machine (`camera-bridge/README.md` section 8). Gap: the portal counted 0 crossings on 10-24 and 10-25 while detections flowed; 8 arrivals vs 33 billed over 10-15..10-21. Find which rule feeds production before writing a new one | Arrivals within 20% of billed jobs over 7 complete ET days |
| Separate a pass-through from a visit | INFERRED: count the crossing first, then classify a visit by queue dwell or a check-in signal; never drop the entry because it failed the visit rule | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/zones/ (loitering_time) | visitd's rule is written (`PASS_THROUGH` = left before ARRIVAL_CANDIDATE, 10 s); the gap is that nobody has measured it against billed jobs | Pass-through share stable week to week |
| Customer wait | INFERRED: name the metric. Lot dwell (entry to exit), arrival-to-bay (entry to bay zone), check-in-to-service (needs a staff or system signal). Publish arrival-to-bay as a camera proxy; call it customer wait only with a check-in and a service-start signal. A lost track is not a departure; an outage is not zero cars | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/review/ (NOT CHECKED by the skeptic) | Waits come from visit rows with no check-in signal | A wait shown without its start and end signal is withdrawn |
| Masks | VERIFIED: motion masks only steer where detection runs; object-filter masks drop a class whose box bottom-centre lands in the mask; over-masking breaks tracking | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/masks/ | Crop only, no per-class masks | Fewer false arrivals on the street lane with no drop in billed match |
| Plates | VERIFIED: Frigate LPR reads plates on the detect stream after a vehicle detection, needs 4 GB RAM and AVX2. RESEARCH: a low-res substream can lack plate detail. A plate read is not an arrival; a repeat plate can be a real second visit (INFERRED) | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/license_plate_recognition/ | No plate read (`plateText` empty on every visit) | Plates on at least 50% of daytime arrivals before any customer join is built |
| Event descriptions | VERIFIED: Frigate GenAI sends several frames of a tracked object to a vision model for text. RESEARCH: CPU-only local GenAI is discouraged | https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/genai/genai_objects/ | Office stills get a summary; lot visits get none | Owner reads them; drop if never opened in 2 weeks |

## Office ears

| Job | Best mechanism | Doc | Our gap | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speech gate | VERIFIED: an RMS energy gate is a pre-gate (Frigate `min_volume`); Silero VAD supports 8/16 kHz at under 1 ms per 30 ms chunk on one thread; whisper-cli has Silero VAD via `--vad` and a model. VERIFIED in source: the whisper.cpp stream example's VAD is `vad_simple`, a relative-energy heuristic | https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad , https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp , https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/master/examples/stream/stream.cpp , https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/audio_detectors/ | A 5 s probe every 15 s (code defaults in `officewake.py` and `install-office-capture.ps1`; the prod env is NOT CHECKED), energy AND peak thresholds, then a whisper run on the probe (speech gate since 10-22) | Missed-speech rate on a labelled hour under 10%, CPU under 30% on the front-desk PC |
| Chunking | INFERRED starting points: 16 kHz mono s16; pre-roll 300-500 ms; endpoint after 600-1000 ms of non-speech; split at 15-25 s with 300-500 ms overlap; one ASR worker; partials off; keep original time offsets and record reconnect gaps | https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper | Fixed 120 s window after a probe wake; words spoken between probes, before the wake, are lost | First words of a conversation present in the transcript |
| ASR worker | INFERRED: one persistent worker (faster-whisper INT8 on CPU, or whisper.cpp `whisper-server`), a bounded queue, capture never waits on ASR; utilisation is about speech duty cycle x RTF. Any spool stays inside the 6 h raw-audio retention | https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper | A new whisper-cli process per clip and per probe (start-up cost each time; probe latency up to 7 s on 10-22) | Probe-to-text p95 under 2 s |
| Diarization | VERIFIED: pyannote Community-1 needs a Hugging Face token and accepted model terms. RESEARCH: it runs on CPU. Belongs after capture, on finished clips | https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio | `speakerCount` NULL (not attempted) | Only if the owner needs "who said it"; never in the capture path |

## Keeping lanes alive

| Job | Best mechanism | Doc | Our gap | Kill criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reader timeout | VERIFIED: the RTSP `timeout` option is the socket I/O timeout in microseconds, set before `-i`. RESEARCH: `-rw_timeout` is reported unreliable for RTSP, `-stimeout` is the older name, `-reconnect*` is HTTP only. Check the installed build with `ffmpeg -h demuxer=rtsp` | https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-protocols.html | No read timeout on any office ffmpeg call; subprocess timeouts only | A hung read ends inside 15 s |
| Progress watchdog | RESEARCH: a reader thread drains stdout and stamps the last PCM time on a monotonic clock; a separate monitor kills and reaps the reader after a startup allowance (20-30 s) or a stall (10-15 s); quiet PCM is healthy; never check the deadline after a blocking read in the same thread; drain stderr | research thread 2026-10-26 07:46 ET | Heartbeat thread beside the work (the 10-22 hang). PR 3298 bounds each capture; PR 3297 pages a stuck one | No lane silent for over 15 min in open hours without a page |
| Supervisor | VERIFIED: WinSW restarts on a nonzero exit with listed delays and a reset window, configured in one XML file; NSSM restarts on exit by default and kills the process tree. RESEARCH: Task Scheduler's restart count and interval apply to a failed task, so a hung process is never restarted (seen live 10-22 to 10-26) | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/winsw/winsw/v3/docs/xml-config-file.md , https://nssm.cc/usage , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/taskschd/tasksettings-restartcount | Office runs as a scheduled task; the lot edge's launcher kills on a 60 s stall (20-73 restarts a day). The repo uses NSSM once (`apps/statenour/local-agent/install-service.ps1`) and has no WinSW config | Restarts per day under 5 with no lost minutes |
| Acceptance tests | RESEARCH list: camera reboot, network loss, silence, quiet speech, TV or music, continuous talk, killed worker, disk full, PC restart and sleep, a long run for thermal throttling | research thread 2026-10-26 07:46 ET | None scripted | Each lane passes the list once before it is called LIVE+PROVEN |
