# Shop retention report contract

## 1. Executive signal
Summarize the strongest retention, repeat-revenue, declined-work, and data-quality findings.

## 2. Data coverage and identity quality
Report exact dates, history window, customer/vehicle/invoice counts, duplicate handling, and missing fields.

## 3. Retention scoreboard
Show new vs returning, repeat-visit cohorts, second-visit timing, dormant cohorts, visit frequency, and repeat revenue share where valid.

## 4. Economics and mix
Show revenue/ARO or equivalent, tire vs service mix, mixed-customer behavior, and category differences without confusing price effects with retention.

## 5. Declined work and reactivation
Quantify open/recovered opportunities and aging bands when data exists.

## 6. Leakage and opportunities
Prioritize identity/data problems, follow-up gaps, one-and-done cohorts, weak cross-sell, and review-request gaps.

## 7. Action queue
Use Recover now, Build habit, Watch. Attach a measurable retention outcome to each action.

## 8. What would change the conclusion
List missing history, contactability, invoice status, service-category, or customer-identity data that matters most.
