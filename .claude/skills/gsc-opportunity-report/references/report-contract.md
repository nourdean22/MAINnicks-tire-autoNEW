# GSC report contract

Use this default structure unless the user requests another format.

## 1. Executive signal
State in 3-6 bullets what materially changed, why it matters, and whether the evidence is strong, mixed, or incomplete.

## 2. Data coverage and integrity
Report property, exact dates, comparison window, filters, dimensions, missing fields, and any comparability limitations.

## 3. KPI scoreboard
Show clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, and the most relevant business-intent segments. Include absolute and percentage deltas when valid.

## 4. Demand map
Group important queries into brand, service/commercial, local, and informational intent. Highlight rising and declining clusters.

## 5. Page and query opportunities
Prioritize no more than 10 items. For each include evidence, likely cause, recommended action, and expected business mechanism.

## 6. Anomalies and risks
Call out tracking/filtering changes, indexing concerns suggested by the data, cannibalization, device/geography divergence, or misleading averages.

## 7. Action queue
Use three buckets: Do now, Test next, Watch. Prefer changes that can be measured in GSC and downstream lead/revenue systems.

## 8. What would change the conclusion
List the missing data or future evidence that would materially alter priorities.
