# Nick's demand report contract

## 1. CEO readout
Give the 5-8 most material facts. State what improved, what worsened, and what remains unknowable.

## 2. Source integrity map
List each source used, exact dates, freshness, and confidence. Highlight tracking gaps before KPI interpretation.

## 3. Demand-chain scoreboard
Use the best available metrics for visibility, engaged demand, leads, bookings, arrivals/completions, revenue, repeats, and reviews. Do not fake absent stages.

## 4. Channel contribution
Summarize organic search/local, direct/brand, paid/social, calls, referrals, repeat business, and other material channels without double-counting.

## 5. Leakage and opportunity
Prioritize the largest bottlenecks and underexploited demand pockets.

## 6. Experiments and operating actions
Use Do now, Prove next, Watch. Give the validation metric and expected mechanism for each action.

## 7. Risks and blind spots
Include data-quality problems, platform dependency, seasonality, capacity constraints, and anything that could make the topline misleading.

## 8. What would change the conclusion
List the next pieces of evidence that would most improve confidence or reorder priorities.
