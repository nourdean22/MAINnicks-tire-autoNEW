## The 4 post archetypes (all you'll ever need)

Every post falls into one of these. **Don't invent new formats.** Pick a template, swap the variables, ship.

### 1. THE PROOF POST — Real customer, real result

**Use:** ~50% of all posts. Highest engagement format.

```
[Customer first name + last initial] needed [specific problem].
Walked in [day/time], left [time-frame] later.
[One concrete number — $ saved, miles back to safe, time finished].

Real review excerpt: "[15-25 word quote — verified Google or photo of receipt]"

Drop in any day, walk-ins welcome.
17625 Euclid Ave · (216) 862-0005
```

**Example (brakes):**
> Marcus L. came in with grinding brakes and a $1,200 dealer quote.
> Walked in Saturday at 11am. Left at 1:30pm.
> $487. Real pads. Real rotors. Real receipt.
>
> "Way cheaper than the dealer wanted, and they showed me the worn part on the lift before they touched it."
>
> Drop in any day, walk-ins welcome.
> 17625 Euclid Ave · (216) 862-0005

**Image:** photo of the actual repaired vehicle in our bay (no stock).

### 2. THE ANTI-POST — Industry honesty (VOICE.md pattern 2)

**Use:** ~15% of posts. Builds trust faster than anything else.

```
We won't [common upsell every shop does].
We won't [common upsell every shop does].
We won't [common upsell every shop does].

Cleveland deserves a shop that earns the bill instead of inflating it.

(216) 862-0005 · open 7 days · 17625 Euclid Ave
```

**Example:**

> We won't add a fee at pickup that wasn't on the written estimate.
>
> Cleveland deserves a shop that earns the bill instead of inflating it.
>
> (216) 862-0005 · open 7 days · 17625 Euclid Ave

**Image:** the storefront sign at golden hour (`brand-sign.webp`) OR a still of the lift with a clearly labeled worn part.

### 3. THE FIRST-PRINCIPLES POST — Math-as-argument (VOICE.md pattern 4)

**Use:** ~15% of posts. Best for financing + preventive-care messages.

```
$[X small number] [thing] today.
$[Y big number] [bigger thing] in 30 days if you wait.

[One-sentence explanation of the cascade.]

We can finance the small fix · $0 down today · pay it down monthly.
(216) 862-0005
```

**Example (brakes cascade):**
> $487 brake job today.
> $2,100 brake + caliper + rotor job in 30 days if pads grind metal.
>
> Cleveland salt eats brake hardware faster than dry-state cars. Catching it early IS the maintenance.
>
> Acima · Snap · Koalafi · $0 down today · pay it down monthly.
> (216) 862-0005

**Image:** a side-by-side photo of (left) a worn pad, (right) a chewed-up rotor — with `$487` and `$2,100` overlaid in our brand yellow.

### 4. THE SEASONAL POST — Cleveland-specific timing

**Use:** ~20% of posts. Anchors us to the local moment.

```
[Cleveland weather/event happening THIS week.]

What it does to your car: [one specific consequence].

Quick fix that costs less than ignoring it: [service we offer].

Open [hours that day] · 17625 Euclid Ave · (216) 862-0005
```

**Example (pothole season — March):**
> First salt-truck week of the year is over. The roads are scarred.
>
> What it does to your car: bent control arms, knocked-out alignment, slow tire-tread cupping you'll feel on I-90.
>
> Quick fix that costs less than ignoring it: free alignment check while you wait — 20 minutes.
>
> Open 7 days · 17625 Euclid Ave · (216) 862-0005

**Image:** an actual Cleveland pothole on a recognizable street (not stock).

## The 1:5 ratio (NO MORE)

**Out of every 5 posts: at most 1 may be a direct sales pitch.** The other 4 are proof, anti-promise, math-as-argument, or seasonal. This is non-negotiable. Social channels punish overt selling — the algorithm + the audience both downvote it.

## The grade-before-ship checklist (before scheduling any post)

1. Would another auto shop in Cleveland write this? If yes → rewrite.
2. Is there a number? (Specific $ amount, time, miles, days)
3. Is there a verb? (Static phrases die)
4. Does it claim something or show something? (Cut "trusted", "expert", "quality")
5. Cliché kill list (`VOICE.md`) — anything caught?
6. Image: real Nick's content or stock? Stock = NO ship.

Pass all six = ship.

## When voice goes too far (specific to social)

- **Stacked jokes in a single post** → cancels itself out. One absurdity max per post.
- **Self-aware "yes I'm doing the thing"** → cringe. The line should land, then move on.
- **Punching down at customers or competitors by name** → never. Anti-shop posts can mention "the chain stores" or "the dealer" — never specific brands.
- **Politics, religion, sports rivalries** → off-limits. Not even Browns/Steelers.
- **Inside jokes nobody who hasn't been to Cleveland gets** → kills shareability.

## The kill list (delete on sight from any post)

Same as `VOICE.md`. Highlights:
- "Trusted" / "Trust us"
- "Expert" / "Experts"
- "Top-quality" / "Premium" (without a specific spec backing it)
- "Family-owned" → replace with "We close at 6 because we have families."
- "Hassle-free" → replace with "Done before your patience runs out."
- "State-of-the-art" → use the actual scanner model number
- "Welcome to..." (visitors didn't ask)
