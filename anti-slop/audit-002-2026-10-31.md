## Findings

1. **HIGH · R-02 em dash.** Six error toasts the chat shows carry an em dash:
   `hooks/use-voice-input.ts:77` "Voice transcription failed — try again", `:163` "Microphone unavailable —
   check permissions", `:246` "Voice transcription failed — try again", `:250` "Voice transcription failed —
   check your connection", `:269` "Couldn't start recording on this device — try again", and
   `hooks/chat/use-chat-transport.ts:398` "Stream decode error — response may be incomplete". The same
   hook's newer lines (132-135, 142) already use a period. Fix: period or colon in all six
   ("Voice transcription failed. Try again."). `use-voice-input` also serves Missions voice dump, so the fix
   reaches that page too.
2. **HIGH · R-27 UI states (my own U6a, PR 3449).** The Archived tab shows one line, "Archived conversations
   not loaded.", both while the read is in flight and after it fails
   (`features/chat-v2/components/operator-conversation-drawer.tsx:119-121`; `hooks/use-conversations.ts:421-427`
   sets `null` for both), and offers no Retry. The main list next to it already has the three states and a
   Retry (`:112-117`). Fix: a loading/error state for the archived read (keep `null` for not-read, add an
   error flag), "Loading archived…" while in flight, "Archived list could not be read." plus Retry on
   failure.
3. **MEDIUM · R-04 icons.** The Context and Evidence panel heads its recall list with Lucide `Sparkles`
   (`components/chat/memory-inspector-sidebar.tsx:234`) and its title with `Brain` (`:110`), both listed
   generic-AI glyphs with no written reason. The chat header already dropped the Brain glyph for the word
   "Context" with exactly this reason (`chat-island.tsx:425`). Fix: drop both icons; the headings say what
   the section is.
4. **MEDIUM · R-04 icons.** Decision citations in a reply use `Sparkles`
   (`components/chat/citation-pills.tsx:56`); the other citation kinds use icons that match their kind. Fix:
   a glyph that reads as a decision (`Scale` or `GitBranch`) with the reason written, or no icon.
5. **LOW · R-06 typography.** Small section labels set in 11 px monospace with 0.12em tracking repeat across
   the chat: `typed-tool-cards.tsx:47,110,123`, `media-saved-moments.tsx:43`, `media-timestamp-bar.tsx:49`,
   `chat-message-list.tsx:557`, and the inspector headings (`memory-inspector-sidebar.tsx:235`). No
   uppercase, and the contrast passes (`--text-tertiary` #8E887C on `--overlay` #101010 is about 5.4:1), but
   mono-plus-tracking as the default label voice is the "terminal" look R-06 asks a written reason for. Fix:
   write the reason once in `tokens.css` (if mono is the deliberate label voice of NOUR OS), or move labels
   to the sans face at 12 px.

## Checked and kept, with the reason written (R-31)

- Contrast: `--text-tertiary` #8E887C on `--overlay` #101010 is about 5.4:1 and on `--canvas` #090907 a little
  higher; the 11-12 px tertiary text in the drawer, inspector and More sheet passes AA (R-25). The app ships one
  dark theme, so R-34 has one mode to hold.
- Tap targets: every chat button found carries `min-h-11` or `h-11 w-11` (44 px); the scan for `h-6`-`h-9`
  buttons found none below that (R-03).
- Keyboard: `app/styles/base.css:54` sets an unlayered `:focus-visible` outline on every button, input and
  textarea, which beats the `outline-none` on the composer textarea and the sheet containers (R-32). The More
  sheet and the drawer are Dialogs (Escape closes, focus returns to the trigger).
- Native dialogs: none in the chat; confirms are in-DOM (CI gate, `nickstire-ios-pwa-primitives`).
- Copy: no generic CTAs, no buzzwords, no emoji in UI strings (R-15, R-16). The U6c pop-up rows say what they
  mute ("Copied, saved, pinned, new chat ready") and state the limit ("Errors and Undo always show.").
- States: the main history list (loading, error with Retry, empty), message search (U6b: under 3 characters,
  searching, "Message search failed", "No messages match") and the recall list (degraded read folds nothing,
  U2) each have their three states (R-27).
- Video (U3): a plain Play button, keyboard reachable, 44 px, no autoplay, no third-party request before the
  tap; Instagram and TikTok get a link card (R-26, R-32).
- Lucide as the icon library: the app uses it everywhere, consistently sized at 16 px in the chat; the glyphs
  outside findings 3 and 4 match their action (History, Mic, Paperclip, Pin, Search) (R-04).
