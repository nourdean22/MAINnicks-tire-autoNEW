Warning Signs

SIGNS YOU NEED COOLING SYSTEM SERVICE

If you notice any of these, do not wait. Bring your vehicle in for an inspection.

1

Temperature gauge reading high or in the red

2

Coolant leak under the vehicle (green, orange, or pink fluid)

3

Sweet smell from the engine or inside the cabin

4

Low coolant warning light

5

Heater blowing cold air in winter

6

Steam coming from under the hood

How We Work

OUR COOLING SYSTEM PROCESS

Every repair follows a structured process. We diagnose first, explain what we find, and fix it right.

01

Pressure Test

Pressurize the cooling system to find any leaks — radiator, hoses, water pump, heater core.

02

Diagnose

Check thermostat operation, water pump flow, radiator condition, and head gasket integrity.

03

Quote

Written estimate for repair. Explain what failed and why.

04

Repair

Replace failed components, flush system if needed, refill with correct coolant, verify operating temperature.
